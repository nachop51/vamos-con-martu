# CONTEXT

Glossary of domain terms for the "Martu en Disney" project (repo name `vamos-marti` is historical; the brand is Martu en Disney).

## Terms

### Martu en Disney (brand)
The brand — matches her existing Instagram identity [@pazmartu.m](https://www.instagram.com/pazmartu.m) ("Martu en Disney"). Site title, logo text, OG tags use this. "**Pero real**" is a recurring saying/tagline (e.g. "Disney, pero real"), NOT part of the brand name. Domain not purchased yet. Logo must NOT imitate Disney's lettering/logo style (trademark); plain descriptive use of the word is the accepted risk.

### Martu (Martina Paz)
Founder and face of the venture; "Martu" is her public nickname (short for Martina). 27, Uruguayan, Licenciada en Comunicación. Has been to Disney Orlando several times; shares rankings, recorridos and trip content on Instagram [@pazmartu.m](https://www.instagram.com/pazmartu.m). Brand personality: Disney, pink, castles, princesses, magical/animated modern aesthetic.

### Disney (destination)
In this project "Disney" means **Walt Disney World, Orlando, Florida** — the 4-park resort (Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom). Not Disneyland California. Positioned broadly as "Orlando" so Universal and other side trips can be added later.

### Jetmar
Travel agency Martina collaborates with. Handles actual travel bookings (flights/packages). Martina is the Disney expertise layer on top; Jetmar is the fulfilment partner. **Not mentioned publicly on the site for now** — copy focuses on Martu and the trip only; generic references ("equipo experto en planificación") are OK. Division of roles: agency = operations (vuelos, hoteles, tickets, gestión comercial/administrativa); Martu = experience design + acompañamiento.

### Consulta (lead)
The site's conversion goal: a visitor reaching out to Martina via WhatsApp or the contact form to ask about a Disney trip. No online payments or scheduled bookings in v1 — every service conversation starts as a Consulta.

### Viajero (audience)
Primary audience: Uruguayans planning a Disney trip — familias, mamás/papás con hijos, grupos de amigas, primer viaje a Disney, adultos fans de Disney, personas que prefieren grupos reducidos. **Explicitly NOT** quinceañeras nor grupos masivos adolescentes (clarified on site as an FAQ item, not hero copy). Open to other Spanish speakers but Uruguay-first. All site copy in Rioplatense Spanish with voseo ("descubrí", "escribime").

### Viaje personalizado (service)
Remote planning service: Martina designs the trip (parks, days, tips) and quotes it with Jetmar; the client travels on their own.

### Salida grupal (service)
Guided group trip: a scheduled departure to Orlando where Martina travels with and accompanies the group. **Lead offering** since Jul 2026. Positioning: "experiencia acompañada" — grupos reducidos, flexibles y personalizados; acompañamiento antes (reuniones pre-viaje, grupo WhatsApp, ayuda con apps), durante (organización sin estrés, descanso, tiempo libre, kits de bienvenida, sorpresas temáticas) y después (fotos y recuerdos). Possible formats: Disney + Universal, Halloween, Navidad, Festivales EPCOT. Site shows **no hard dates** — formats listed as possibilities, CTA is a [[consulta]].

### Venture
Martina's paid Disney trip-guidance business. Early stage — just launching, no track record to show yet. Not a free-content site — the site exists to convert visitors into paying clients / booking leads. **Salida grupal is the lead offering** (pivot, Jul 2026); Viaje personalizado is secondary.
